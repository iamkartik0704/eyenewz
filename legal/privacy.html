Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

App: EyeNewz (com.prod.contentnews)

Operator: EyeNewz

Summary

We collect minimal data. No account is required. Article text is cached on your device. We use Firebase Crashlytics for anonymous crash reports on release builds.

Data stored on your device

  • Cached article headlines, summaries, and metadata (offline reading)
  • Your preferences: hidden categories, blocked publishers/keywords, saved articles

Data sent to our servers

  • API requests to fetch the news feed (no personal identity attached in V1)

Third parties

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash logs, device model, OS version (release builds only)
  • Original publishers — when you tap “Open original source”

Data we do not collect

  • Name, email, or phone (unless you email us)
  • Precise location
  • Advertising ID for ad targeting
  • Sale of personal data

Children

Not directed at children under 13.

Contact & deletion

Email support@eyenewz.com. Uninstalling the app removes local data.
